# Dashboard README

The Tableau dashboard visualises the final opportunity output and helps users explore company priority, financial signal structure, cluster profiles and forward-looking indicators.

The dashboard contains five views D1-D5.

## D1: Opportunity Overview

D1 provides the overall summary of the opportunity scoring output. 

It shows the total company count, complete score companies, top-decile opportunities, median priority score, fresh accounts coverage and high-confidence assignments. 

The main visual is the Cluster Opportunity Matrix, which compares financial clusters by company count, completion rate, median priority score and primary opportunity dimensions. It also includes the primary dimension mix and priority score distribution across Deepen, Defend and Grow.

## D2: Opportunity List

D2 focuses on the distribution and separation of opportunity dimensions. 

It shows the percentile distributions for Deepen, Relative Grow and Defend scores, together with the relationship between primary and secondary dimensions. The score-versus-coverage chart helps assess whether high-priority companies have sufficient evidence coverage. The primary separation chart shows how clearly companies are assigned to a dominant opportunity dimension.

## D3: Cluster Profiles

D3 explains the financial cluster structure behind the opportunity scores. 

It summarises the number of cluster-assigned companies, completion rate, global versus cluster percentiles, and the distribution of Deepen, Defend and Grow companies within each cluster. It also shows how evidence tiers vary across clusters. This view helps users understand whether a company’s opportunity score is linked to its broader financial archetype.

## D4: Top 20 Priority Company Explorer

D4 is designed for company-level inspection. It allows users to filter by company name and review high-priority companies in detail. 

The dashboard shows coverage indicators, calibrated priority score, queue percentile, queue rank, margin value and accounts age. It also displays company details, reason codes, cluster assignment confidence, global-versus-peer comparisons and dimension-level percentile comparisons. This view is closest to the relationship manager use case.

## D5: Forward Signal Overview

D5 focuses on forward-looking financial signals. It summarises the number of companies with forward signal data, complete records and top-20% groups for Deepen, Relative Grow and Defend. 

The dashboard includes forward signal details by company, companies by scale tier, companies by sector, sector-by-scale concentration, direction coverage and scale combinations. This view helps users assess how future-oriented financial movement differs across sectors and company scale groups.
